About Me

My name is Ebenezer Emmanuel, but most people call me Peter. I am a beginner web developer based in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria.

My Story

I started learning web development because I believe technology is the most powerful tool to change my life and the lives of people around me. I want to build things that matter, websites, applications, and eventually AI products that solve real problems for real people.

I am currently going through freeCodeCamp's Responsive Web Design Certification, learning HTML and CSS from scratch. Every day I write code, take handwritten notes, build projects, and push my work to GitHub so the world can see my progress.

I believe consistency beats talent. I show up every day even when it is hard. And I know that the journey from HTML to AI Engineering takes years, but I am committed to walking every step of that road.
